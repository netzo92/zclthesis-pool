Use your own payout address

The ZCL address you enter when mining will receive your payouts. The 0.8% fee leaves 99.2% of allocated rewards for miners. Rewards are proportional to accepted work in each pool round. Eligible balances are paid at 0.05 ZCL after coinbase maturity and shielding confirmations; the pool covers transaction fees from its operator reserve. Accepted shares do not guarantee a block or a payment.
